Engineering Blueprint

Under The Surface

Powertrain

Tri-motor electric architecture featuring one high-efficiency permanent magnet synchronous motor per rear wheel and a single front axle motor. Combined output: 1,200 kW (1,632 PS) with torque vectoring across the rear axle for precision cornering at any speed.

Battery System

200 kWh solid-state lithium-sulfur cells arranged in a structural floor pack. 800V architecture enables 350 kW DC fast charging — 10% to 80% in 12 minutes. Predicted cycle life exceeds 2,000 full charges with less than 8% degradation.

Chassis

Full carbon fiber monocoque with integrated roll protection. Dry weight: 1,298 kg. Front and rear subframes are machined from billet aluminum. Active suspension with predictive road scanning via front-facing LiDAR.

Aerodynamics

Active aerodynamic surfaces include a deployable rear wing generating 820 kg of downforce at 300 km/h, front splitter with three-position adjustment, and underbody diffuser channels. Cd 0.208 in low-drag mode, Cd 0.34 in maximum downforce configuration.

Braking

Carbon-ceramic discs (420 mm front, 390 mm rear) with 8-piston front calipers. Regenerative braking provides up to 400 kW of recuperation. Emergency braking from 100–0 km/h in 28 meters with pedestrian detection override.

Connectivity

Full over-the-air update capability for powertrain calibration, suspension mapping, and aerodynamic profiles. 5G telemetry with real-time track analytics. Vehicle-to-vehicle communication for platoon efficiency on controlled corridors.
